A Physician's Map · 2026

The Medical Malpractice Climate, by State

A composite of noneconomic damage caps, premium levels for high-risk specialties, claim frequency, and verdict severity. Where you practice is not a footnote — it's a multi-decade financial decision.

Favorable — meaningful caps, low premiums, predictable
Mixed — partial reform or moderate climate
Hostile — weak/no caps, high premiums, nuclear verdict territory
Climate ratings reflect the combined picture of state cap structure (or absence thereof), 2025 manual premium levels for OB/GYN and general surgery, plaintiff-friendliness of urban venues, recent verdict trends, and Certificate-of-Merit / pre-suit screening rigor. A "Mixed" rating typically reflects either partial caps that have eroded with inflation, recent legal whiplash, or a state where climate varies sharply between metro and non-metro counties. Sources: AMA Medical Liability Monitor 2025 Survey · ATRA Judicial Hellholes 2025–26 · NCSL state cap registry · State medical society reports

How This Works

Composite score = cap structure (0–35) + NAIC premium burden per physician (0–25) + ATRA litigation environment (0–15). Lower scores indicate friendlier climates for physicians.

Premium per physician = NAIC most-recent-year direct premium written ÷ AAMC active physicians. This is the total medical professional liability premium dollars flowing through a state's market, divided by the number of practicing physicians who could be its policyholders. It's a system-level metric — not a manual rate — but it's a real, primary-source measurement of the financial weight of malpractice insurance in each state.

Annual update workflow: When NAIC publishes the new vintage of "Countrywide Summary of Medical Professional Liability Insurance" (typically 18 months after year-end), download the PDF, run the parsing script, and commit the updated map_data.json. The dashboard renders the new data automatically. No scraping; no third-party APIs; no licensing fees.
